MAX+ is a fast, lightweight and privacy-focused open source client for the MAX messaging platform.

Key Features:
• Two-Layer End-to-End Encryption: Protects messages and calls using modern cryptographic protocols (x25519, ChaCha20-Poly1305).
• Zero Google Play Services Dependency: Fully functional standalone background connection for reliable real-time pushless messaging and incoming call notifications.
• Audio and Video Calls: High-quality peer-to-peer and group calls with screen wake-up support.
• Multi-Account & Biometric Lock: Keep your data secure with biometric encryption keys.
• Free & Open Source: Licensed under GPL-3.0 with complete transparency and zero tracking.
